# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-08)

### Capture

- Snag an area dragged over the page, what the window shows, or the whole
  page.
- Snag after a countdown, to capture menus and hover states.
- Elements cut off by scrolling containers, and picked scrolling containers,
  come out whole.
- Pick inside open shadow roots and same-origin frames.
- Choose between screen and CSS resolution. Snags past the size limit say
  they were scaled down.

### Library

- Open snags full size, and copy them as Markdown, a data URL or a link to
  their page.
- Mark snags up with arrows, rectangles, text and blur, and crop them.
- Search snags, filter them by site and delete the ones shown.
- Undo deletions for five seconds.
- The popup stays where you drag it.

### Downloads

- Download as PNG, JPEG or WebP, into a folder, or with a Save As dialog.

### Everything else

- An options page, reached from the popup.
- Argentinian Spanish.
- A light, dark or system theme.
- The toolbar icon shows `!` on pages Snag cannot run on.
- A full disk gets its own message.
- Copy diagnostics for bug reports.
- Firefox 127 or later.
